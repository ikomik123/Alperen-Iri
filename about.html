About

I teach children the way that would have worked for me when I was their age: using visual examples and staying energetic. Back when I began working as a teacher's assistant in elementary school, I had a teacher who constantly forced her opinions onto everything. In later years, I encountered teachers who were only interested in their paycheck rather than teaching, which made the classes incredibly boring.

Because of this, there is no room for my personal opinion during my classes; instead, I teach everything using physical or visual examples. This ranges from showing an actual computer part to using a picture or video. I also make an effort to connect with my students by getting to know them before or after class, or by playing games with them.
